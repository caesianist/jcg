The name given in the Book of Genesis to the region where the binding of [[Isaac]] by [[Abraham]] is said to have taken place. Jews identify the region mentioned in Genesis and the specific mountain in which the event is said to have occurred with "Mount Moriah", mentioned in the Book of Chronicles as the place where [[Solomon's Temple]] is said to have been built, and both these locations are also identified with the current Temple Mount in Jerusalem, though some translations render the location of the binding of Isaac as being [[Moreh]] instead.
