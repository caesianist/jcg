Bishop of Rome from around 92 to 100 AD, succeeded by [[Evaristus]]. [[Tertullian]] claimed that Clement was ordained by [[Peter the Apostle]]. Early church lists place him as the second or third bishop of Rome. [[Eusebius of Caesarea]] mentioned Clement as the third bishop of Rome and as the "co-laborer" of [[Paul the Apostle]]. [[Irenaeus]] described Clement as the successor to [[Anacletus]], who was the third bishop of Rome, and as a personal acquaintance of the Apostles.
