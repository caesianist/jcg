The name of some group that engaged in a conflict with [[Judah]] in [[Testament of Judah]].

Machir was the son of [[Manasseh]], grandson of [[Joseph]], and father of [[Gilead]] in [[Numbers 26]].

According to [[1 Samuel]] and [[2 Samuel]], Machir, son of [[Ammiel]] was the name of a descendant of Machir ben Manasseh who resided at Lo-debar. The text states that here he looked after [[Mephibosheth]], the son of [[Jonathan]], until [[David]] took over his care, and also looked after David himself when David became a fugitive.
