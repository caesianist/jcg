Daughter of [[Eve]], becomes the target of the archons, particularly the chief archon. The archon attempts to seduce/possess her.  Norea rejects him, asserting that she belongs to the higher divine realm rather than to the archons. She then burns down the ark that the archons had commanded Noah to construct. She calls upon the Most High God, who sends [[Eleleth]], one of the great heavenly powers, to rescue and instruct her. Eleleth reveals to Norea the true nature of the archons and the higher divine order.
