Child of [[Sheshan]], despite text saying son of, verses later it says Sheshan only had daughters, so it is likely Ahlai was simply the heir of Sheshan and thus called a "son of" Sheshan. We can likely say she was the heir as the other daughters Sheshan has, was with his Egyptian slave, [[Jarha]], and thus the children of this slave likely would've been defaulted as not being an heir.
