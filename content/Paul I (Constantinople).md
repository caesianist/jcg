Sixth bishop of Constantinople, elected first in 337. Paul I became involved in the controversy around [[Arius]] which drew in the Emperor of the West, Constans, and his counterpart in the East, his brother Roman emperor [[Constantius II]]. Paul I was installed and deposed three times from the See of Constantinople between 337 and 350. He was murdered by strangulation during his third and final exile in Cappadocia. Succeeded by [[Macedonius I]].
