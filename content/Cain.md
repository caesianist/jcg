Mentioned in [[Genesis 4]]. Son of [[Adam]] and [[Eve]], brothers with [[Abel]]. He was a farmer who gave an offering of his crops to God. However, God was not pleased and favored Abel's offering over Cain's. Out of jealousy, Cain killed his brother, for which he was punished by God with the curse and mark of Cain. He had several descendants, starting with his son [[Enoch]] and including [[Lamech]].
