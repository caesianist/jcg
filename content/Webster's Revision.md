[[Noah Webster]], whose name is synonymous with the term “dictionary,” produced a limited revision of the [[King James Version]], mainly to update some of the more archaic language. He made very few changes, and the changes he did make were generally limited to English vocabulary and grammar, rather than translation improvements. There are two notable exceptions. The first was changing “Easter” in Acts 12 to “Passover,” as the KJV translators had erred. The second was replacing “Holy Ghost” with “Holy Spirit,” as common English usage of ‘ghost’ suggested something other than God Himself.
