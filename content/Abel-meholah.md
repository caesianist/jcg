Located in the Jordan valley near the Jordan river. After [[Gideon]]'s army of 300 men defeated the [[Midianites]], the enemy fled, and the pursuit extended to Abel-meholah, in [[1 Kings 4]], this area is also noted as part of the administrative district under the jurisdiction of [[Baana]], one of [[Solomon]]'s twelve district governors. [[Elisha]] was also anointed as a prophet in place of [[Elijah]] here.
