Member of the [[Levites]], son of [[Berechiah]] one of the leaders of [[David]]'s choir and a "seer"; [[Psalms 50]] and 73–83 are attributed to him. He was also the father of [[Zaccur]], [[Joseph]], [[Nethaniah]], and [[Asarelah]].

Father of [[Joah]], the recorder for [[Hezekiah]]; possibly the same as the [[Asaph]] above.

The "keeper of the king's forest" under [[Artaxerxes I]], to whom [[Nehemiah]] requested a letter authorizing timber for the temple in Jerusalem.
