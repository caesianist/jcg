Born Saul of Tarsus. Christian apostle who spread the teachings of [[Jesus]] in the first century AD. For his contributions towards the New Testament, he is generally regarded as one of the most important figures of the Apostolic Age, and he also founded several Christian communities in Asia Minor and Europe from the mid-40s to the mid-50s AD. 

He wrote numerous texts for the bible:

[[Epistle to the Galatians]] c. 48–55 AD
[[1 Corinthians]]  c. 53–55 AD
[[2 Corinthians]] c. 55–56 AD
[[Epistle to the Romans]] c. 56–58 AD
[[Epistle to the Philippians]] c. 60–62 AD
[[Epistle to Philemon]] c. 60–62 AD
