Following [[William Tyndale]]'s execution, King [[Henry VIII]] promised an authorized version of the Bible. Because work on the later-published Great Bible was slow, an interim work was published. Working under the false name [[Thomas Matthew]], [[John Rogers]] combined Tyndale’s New Testament, a combination of Tyndale’s and [[Myles Coverdale]]'s Old Testament, and his own translation of the [[Prayer of Manasseh]] (an apocryphal work).
