Deity in the pantheon of [[Canaan]] known as [[Astarte]]. She was connected with some combination of the following spheres: war, sexuality, royal power, beauty, healing and hunting. Her symbol was the lion and she was also often associated with the horse and by extension chariots. The dove might be a symbol of her as well. The bible depicts certain groups as worshipping "the Baals and Astartes" so this means Astartes is meant to represent a number of deities similar to Astarte.
