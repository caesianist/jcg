Also known as [[Jehuel]].

Can refer to:

Member of the [[Levites]], musician in the time of [[David]].

The leader of a family of [[Gershonites]] in the time of David, custodian of "the treasury of the house of the Lord."

Son of [[Hachmoni]], who was with David's sons.

Son of king [[Jehoshaphat]].

Member of the [[Hemanites]] in the time of [[Hezekiah]], sometimes known as Jehuel.

Levitical or priestly overseer of the temple in the time of Hezekiah.

A person referred to as "ruler of the house of God" in the time of king [[Josiah]]. 

The father of [[Obadiah]] in a post-exilic list of kin groups.

The father of [[Shecaniah]].

The son of [[Harim]], a priest (Ezra 10:21).

Son of [[Elam]].

Son of [[Ladan]].
