Ancient and biblical name for a mountainous region stretching between the Dead Sea and the Gulf of Aqaba in the northwestern region of [[Edom]], southeast of the Kingdom of [[Judah]]. It may also have marked the older historical limit of Ancient Egypt in [[Canaan]].
