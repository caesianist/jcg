The first of the two Crusades led by Louis IX of France. It aimed to reclaim the Holy Land by attacking Egypt, the main Islamic power in the Near East. The Crusade was conducted in response to setbacks in the Kingdom of Jerusalem, beginning with the loss of the Holy City in 1244, and was preached by Innocent IV in conjunction with a crusade against emperor [[Frederick II]], Baltic rebellions and Mongol incursions. After initial success, the crusade ended in defeat, with most of the army including the king captured by the Egyptians. After this was the [[Eighth Crusade]].
