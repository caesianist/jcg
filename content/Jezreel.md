The scene of several battles with the [[Israelites]]. A victory was led by [[Deborah]] and [[Barak]] against the [[Canaanites]]. Another was led by [[Gideon]] against the [[Midianites]] and the [[Amalekites]]. It was later the location at which the Israelites, led by King [[Saul]], were defeated by the [[Philistines]]. Was also where [[Jehu]] massacred all members of the [[Omrides]]. 

Member of the tribe of [[Judah]], son of [[Etam]].

Son of [[Gomer]] and [[Hosea]].
