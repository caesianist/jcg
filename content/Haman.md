The main antagonist in the [[Book of Esther]], was a proud and ambitious man who demanded that everyone bow down to him as a sign of respect. However, a Jewish man named [[Mordecai]] refused to bow down to him, which enraged Haman. Seeking revenge, Haman convinced the king to issue a decree that all Jews in the Persian empire be exterminated. Haman's plot was foiled by [[Esther]], who was also Jewish and had concealed her identity from the King. Esther revealed Haman's plan to [[Xerxes I]] and pleaded with him to spare her people. The King was outraged at Haman's treachery and ordered that he be executed instead.
