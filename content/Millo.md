A structure in Jerusalem referred to in the Hebrew Bible, first mentioned as being part of the city of [[David]] in [[2 Samuel 5]]:9. However it previously seems to have been a rampart built by the [[Jebusites]] prior to Jerusalem's being conquered by the [[Israelites]]. The texts also describe the Millo built by [[Solomon]] and repaired by [[Hezekiah]], without giving an explanation of what exactly the Millo was: there is therefore some debate as to the nature of what Millo was.
