Another name for [[Sophia]]. A heavenly/divine being who precedes the creation of the material cosmos. She is a heavenly being/Eon existing in the higher divine realm. She observes the chaos below and creates or produces a being from herself, called [[Saklas]] ("fool") one of the chief archons. Saklas is ignorant of the higher divine reality and declares, essentially, “I am God and there is no other God beside me.” Pistis then reveals the existence of the higher God and mocks the archon's ignorance. She ultimately brings about the downfall of the archons and the liberation of the spiritual human race.
