Anatolian Indo-European people who formed one of the first major civilizations of the Bronze Age in West Asia. The Bible refers to people as "Hittites" in several passages. The relationship between these peoples and the Bronze Age Hittite Empire is unclear. In some passages, the Biblical Hittites appear to have their own kingdoms, apparently located outside geographic [[Canaan]], and were powerful enough to defeat Syrian armies in battle. In these passages, the Biblical Hittites appear to refer to the Iron Age Syro-Hittite states. However, in most of their appearances, the Biblical Hittites are depicted as a people living among the [[Israelites]].
