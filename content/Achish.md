A name used for two Philistine rulers of [[Gath]]. It is perhaps only a general title of royalty, applicable to the Philistine kings. The two kings of Gath, which most scholars identify are:

The monarch, described as "Achish the king of Gath", with whom [[David]] sought refuge when he fled from Saul. He is called [[Abimelech]].

Another king of Gath, described as "Achish, son of [[Maoch]]," probably a grandson of the foregoing king, is referred to during [[Solomon]]'s reign.
