Christian theologian and Patriarch of Alexandria from 412 to 444. Cyril wrote extensively and was a major player in the Christological controversies of the late 4th and 5th centuries. He was the President of the [[First Council of Ephesus]] in 431, which clarified the hypostatic union of Christ's divine and human natures into one person against the views of [[Nestorius]], who was deposed as Patriarch of Constantinople. He is also well-known for his advocacy of the term Theotokos for the Virgin Mary, which followed from his Christological views.
