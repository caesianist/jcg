Can either refer to a few figures in the Old or New Testament Figure.

In the Old Testament:
The son of [[Jacob]] and [[Rachel]]. He is eventually sold into slavery by his brothers to [[Ishmaelites]], his story starts in [[Genesis 37]] and ends in [[Genesis 50]].
The father of [[Igal]].
Son of [[Asaph]].

In the New Testament:
The 1st-century Jewish carpenter of Nazareth who was married to [[Mary]], and was the legal father of [[Jesus]].
Man from [[Arimathea]] who is a rich man who donated his tomb to receive the body of Jesus.
Son of [[Mary]] and [[Clopas]] and brother of [[James the Just]].
Father of [[Jannai]], son of [[Mattathias]].
Father of [[Judah]].
Person later named [[Barnabas]], native to [[Cyprus]].
Son of [[Zechariah]].
