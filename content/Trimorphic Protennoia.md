2nd/3rd century Gnostic text. The text describes three descents using the voice of [[Barbelo]] in first person. The voice is the source of life, knowledge, and the first thought. The voice is said to have three names, three masculinities, and three powers, and it is described as androgynous. It explains that [[Sophia]] descended to help counter the plan of the demon [[Yaldabaoth]] and the [[Archons]].
