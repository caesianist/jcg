Christological church synod in 449 convened by Emperor [[Theodosius II]] in [[Ephesus]] under the presidency of Pope [[Dioscorus I]]. It was intended to be an ecumenical council, and it is accepted by the miaphysite churches, who view it as a valid continuation of the [[First Council of Ephesus]] if not an ecumenical council in its own right. The [[Second Council of Ephesus]] was explicitly repudiated by the next council, the [[Council of Chalcedon]] of 451. There was also the [[Third Council of Ephesus]], much less popular and influential.
