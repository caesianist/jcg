The twenty-first and most recent ecumenical council of the Catholic Church. It met in St. Peter's Basilica in Vatican City from 11 October 1962 to 8 December 1965, during the pontificates of Pope [[John XXIII]] and Pope [[Paul VI]]. John XXIII convoked the council as part of a programme of renewal often associated with the term aggiornamento, meaning "bringing up to date". The council addressed the Church's internal life, its relationship with other Christians and non-Christian religions, and its engagement with the modern world.
