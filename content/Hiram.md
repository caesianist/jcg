A king of [[Tyre]]. Hiram's relationship with Israel began during the reign of King [[David]]. In [[2 Samuel 5]], it is recorded, "Now Hiram king of Tyre sent envoys to David, along with cedar logs, carpenters, and stonemasons, and they built a palace for David." He also collaborated with King [[Solomon]] in the construction of [[Solomon's Temple]] in Jerusalem. [[1 Kings 5]] details the agreement between Hiram and Solomon, where Hiram provided cedar and cypress timber, as well as skilled laborers. In return, Solomon supplied Hiram with wheat and olive oil and later in [[1 Kings 9]] gives him cities around Galilee, but Hiram being dissatisfied called the area [[Cabul]].
