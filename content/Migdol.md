Can refer to:

A place near the Red Sea where the [[Israelites]] encamped in [[Exodus 14]].

In [[Joshua 19]], called [[Migdal-Gad]], 'tower of Gad', one of the fortified cities of [[Judah]], and also to [[Migdal-El]], 'tower of God', one of the fortified towns of [[Naphtali]].

In [[Jeremiah 44]], near-geographical relation to [[Tahpanhes]] and [[Memphis]], Egyptian cities where the Jewish people settled in 587 BC.
