 bishop of Rome from 21 July 230 to 28 September 235. In 235, during the persecution of Christians, Pontian was arrested and sent to the island of Sardinia. He abdicated to make the election of a new pope possible. Resigning on 28 September 235, he was the first pope to do so. This allowed an orderly transition in the Church of Rome and so ended a schism that had existed in the Church for eighteen years. Succeeded by [[Anterus]].
