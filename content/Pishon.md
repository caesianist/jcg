One of four rivers identified in [[Genesis 2]]. The Pishon has never been clearly located. It is briefly mentioned together with the [[Tigris]] in [[Sirach 24]], but this reference throws no more light on the location of the river. The Jewish–Roman historian [[Flavius Josephus]], in the beginning of his Antiquities of the Jews (1st century AD) identified the Pishon with the Ganges. The medieval French rabbi [[Rashi]] identified it with the Nile.
