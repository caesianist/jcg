Claimed to be written by "Rabbi Ishmael" who became a "high priest" after visions of ascension to Heaven. This has been taken as referring to [[Rabbi Ishmael]], a third-generation Tanna and a leading figure of Merkabah mysticism. However, he could not have been a High Priest of Israel, as he was born after the Siege of Jerusalem and associated destruction of the Second Temple in 70 CE. An alternative attribution would be the earlier Tanna [[Ishmael ben Elisha]], who lived through the Siege of Jerusalem.
