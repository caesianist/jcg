A city cited in the [[Testament of Levi]], it is the place where [[Levi]] received a vision of the seven heavens. The name is the Greek form of the Hebrew "Abel-Meholah," mentioned in [[Judges 7]] and in [[1 Kings 4]]. Abel-Meholah was situated in the mountains of [[Ephraim]], a fact which calls into question the text of Testament of Levi which associates it with the Sirion mountain ("the mountain of the shield," a false etymology from Shiryon). In the Dead Sea fragment of Testament of Levi, however, the place where Levi received the vision is Abel-Main, an alternate form of Abel-Maim. Abel-Maim is in fact situated in the very northern part of Palestine and could easily be connected with the Sirion. The confusion in Greek Testament of Levi is evidently the translator's.
