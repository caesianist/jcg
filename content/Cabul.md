In [[1 Kings 9]], it is recorded that [[Solomon]] gave [[Hiram]] twenty cities in the land of Galilee as a gesture of gratitude for the cedar and cypress timber, as well as gold, that Hiram had provided for the construction of the Temple and Solomon's palace. However, when Hiram came to inspect the cities, he was displeased with them and called the area "Cabul," which suggests dissatisfaction.
