Later renamed to Israel by God after Jacob wrestled with an angel in [[Genesis 32]]. Son of [[Isaac]] and [[Rebekah]]. With [[Leah]] he had the sons [[Reuben]], [[Simeon]], [[Levi]], [[Judah]], [[Issachar]], [[Zebulun]], and then a daughter called [[Dinah]]. With [[Rachel]] he had the sons [[Joseph]] and [[Benjamin]]. With [[Bilhah]], had the sons [[Dan]] and [[Naphtali]]. With [[Zilpah]], had the sons [[Gad]] and [[Asher]].

In Mormonism, the younger brother of the prophet [[Nephi]], the keeper of the small plates of Nephi after Nephi's death, and narrates the [[Book of Jacob]].

Father of [[Joseph]].
