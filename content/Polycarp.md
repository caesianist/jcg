Christian bishop of Smyrna. According to some traditions, he died a martyr, bound and burned at the stake, then stabbed when the fire failed to consume his body. Both [[Irenaeus]] and [[Tertullian]] say that Polycarp had been a disciple of [[John the Apostle]], one of [[Jesus]]'s disciples, [[Jerome of Stridon]] similarly writes that Polycarp was a disciple of John the Apostle, who had ordained him as a bishop of Smyrna. Polycarp is regarded as one of three chief Apostolic Fathers, along with [[Clement I]] and [[Ignatius of Antioch]].
