Christian writer and the Patriarch of [[Antioch]]. Died somewhere around 108 AD. While en route to Rome, where he was to be killed, Ignatius wrote a series of letters. This correspondence forms a central part of a later collection of works by the Apostolic Fathers. He is considered one of the three most important of these, together with [[Clement I]] and [[Polycarp]]. Although the authenticity of his letters has been questioned.
