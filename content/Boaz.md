Son of [[Salmon]], also known as [[Salma]]. Boaz was a wealthy landowner of Bethlehem in Judea, and relative of [[Elimelech]], [[Naomi]]'s late husband. He notices [[Ruth]], the widowed Moabite daughter-in-law of [[Naomi]], a relative of his  gleaning grain in his fields. He soon learns of the difficult circumstances her family is in and Ruth's loyalty to Naomi. In response, Boaz invites her to eat with him and his workers, as well as deliberately leaving grain for her to claim while keeping a protective eye on her.

One of the two bronze pillars erected by King [[Solomon]] at the entrance of the Temple in Jerusalem.
