Leader of the Tribe of [[Judah]] who became the second king of the united Kingdom of Israel, according to the Hebrew Bible, David's reign may have occurred around the 9th century BC, although the timeframe of his reign and geographical boundaries of his kingdom are disputed. He was annointed king at [[Hebron]]. Father of king [[David]], brother of [[Zeruiah]]. He is also the man who defeated [[Goliath]] as a child.
