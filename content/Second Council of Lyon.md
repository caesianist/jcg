Fourteenth ecumenical council of the Roman Catholic Church, convoked on 31 March 1272 and convened in Lyon, Kingdom of Arles (in modern France), in 1274. Pope [[Gregory X]] presided over the council, called to act on a pledge by Byzantine emperor [[Michael VIII]] to reunite the Eastern church with the West. The council was attended by about 300 bishops, 60 abbots and more than a thousand prelates or their procurators among whom were the representatives of the universities. After this was the [[Council of Vienne]].
