Located near the wilderness of [[Maon]], south of [[Hebron]]. This area is called such as [[David]], along with his men, was hiding in the wilderness of Maon when [[Saul]] and his forces were closing in on them. At a critical moment, a messenger came to [[Saul]], informing him that the [[Philistines]] were raiding the land, prompting Saul to break off his pursuit of David to address the Philistine threat.
