Tenth ecumenical council recognized by the Catholic Church. It was convened by Pope [[Innocent II]] in April 1139 and attended by close to a thousand clerics. Its immediate task was to neutralise the after-effects of the schism which had arisen after the death of Pope [[Honorius II]] in 1130 and the papal election that year that established Pietro Pierleoni as the antipope [[Anacletus II]]. After this the next council was [[Third Council of the Lateran]].
