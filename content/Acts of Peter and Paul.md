4th/5th century apocryphal text. The text is framed as the tale of [[Paul the Apostle]]'s journey from the island of "[[Gaudomeleta]]" (probably Gozo) to Rome, where it also claims that on the way the ship also lands in [[Melita]]. It assigns [[Peter the Apostle]] as Paul's brother. It also describes the death of Paul by beheading.
