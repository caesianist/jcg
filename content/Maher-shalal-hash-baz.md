The child Maher-shalal-hash-baz is the second prophetic-name child after the birth of [[Immanuel]], this name could either be the fulfillment of a prophecy about Immanuel, or understood as son of [[Abi]] the bride of king [[Ahaz]], i.e., the future king [[Hezekiah]], by many Jewish commentators. The phrases maher-shalal and hash-baz are synonymous, both meaning approximately "quickly to the plunder". The name Maher-shalal-hash-baz is a reference to the impending plunder of Samaria and Damascus by the king of Assyria, [[Tiglath-pileser]].
