A hill in Jerusalem, can refer to 3 locations:

Name given to a fortified city of the [[Jebusites]] on the lower part of ancient Jerusalem's Eastern Hill.

Once the First Temple was erected at the top of the Eastern Hill, the name "Mount Zion" migrated there too.

The last shift of the name Mount Zion was to the Western Hill, which is more dominant than the Eastern Hill and seemed to first-century CE.
