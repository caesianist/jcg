Fifth ecumenical councils recognized by both the Eastern Orthodox Church and the Catholic Church. Constantinople II was convoked by the Byzantine Emperor [[Justinian I]] under the presidency of Patriarch [[Eutychius]] of Constantinople. It was held from 5 May to 2 June 553. Participants were overwhelmingly Eastern bishops, only sixteen Western bishops were present, including nine from Illyricum and seven from Africa, but none from Italy out of the 152 total. This council attempted to reconcile Chalcedonian and non-Chalcedonian Christianity. After this was [[Third Council of Constantinople]].
