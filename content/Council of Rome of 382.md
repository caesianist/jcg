A Christian synod convened in Rome in the year 382 AD, under the leadership of Pope [[Damasus I]], being, at the time, the bishop of Rome. It is widely held that this council initially set a Biblical canon in a decree (that is, the Decretum Gelasianum, or "[[Gelasian Decree]]") which is identical to the list of the [[Council of Trent]], though the historicity of this notion has been brought into question.
