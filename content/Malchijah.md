Also known as [[Malchiah]]. Can refer to:

A priest of ancient Israel of the order of [[Aaron]] during the reign of King [[David]] in the 10th century BCE.

Son of [[Harim]], a worker with [[Nehemiah]] and [[Eliashib]] on the repairs to the walls of Jerusalem. 

Son of [[Rechab]], was another worker with Nehemiah and Eliashib on the Jerusalem walls repair. 

A goldsmith working with Nehemiah and Eliashib.

One of those who stood with [[Ezra]] during the reading of the law in [[Nehemiah 8]]. 

Nehemiah's assistant during the dedication service of the restored walls of Jerusalem.
