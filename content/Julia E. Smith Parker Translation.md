The daughter of a minister, [[Julia E. Smith]] was disappointed when [[William Miller]]’s prediction of [[Jesus]]’ return didn’t happen as expected. She believed that was due to straying from the original languages of the Bible, so she made her own. At this writing, it’s the only complete, unaided translation of the Bible by a woman. Because Smith translated very literally, using strict word-for-word techniques, her work was very difficult to read. She took eight years to complete it, but didn’t seek publication for 21 years. At the age of 84, she had 1,000 copies printed… at a retail price of $2.50 each.
