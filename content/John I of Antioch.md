Patriarch of Antioch (429 – 441). He led a group of moderate Eastern bishops during the Nestorian controversy. John I gave active support to his friend [[Nestorius]] in the latter's dispute with [[Cyril of Alexandria]]. In the year 431, he arrived too late for the opening meeting of the [[First Council of Ephesus]]. Cyril, suspecting John I of using procrastinating tactics to support Nestorius, decided not to wait and convened the council without John I and his supporters, condemning Nestorius. When John I reached Ephesus a few days after the council had begun, he convened a counter-council that condemned Cyril and vindicated Nestorius.
