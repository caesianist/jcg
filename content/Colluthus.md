A presbyter (priest) of Alexandria in the early 4th century. He initially opposed [[Arius]] and actually signed [[Alexander of Alexandria]]'s condemnation of Arius. However, Colluthus became dissatisfied with Alexander's handling of the Arian controversy. He separated from Alexander's authority and began acting as a bishop, ordaining priests and deacons without authorization. A later Alexandrian synod invalidated his ordinations, reducing those he had ordained to lay status.
