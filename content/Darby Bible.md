[[John Nelson Darby]] led a team that published a translation of the New Testament in 1867, and his students published an Old Testament translation based on his French and German translations in 1890. Darby’s work was not intended to be read aloud, but was only for study. Considered the father of modern dispensationalism, Darby popularized the theory of a pre-tribulation rapture. His translation decisions were questioned for being biased.
