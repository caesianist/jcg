Can be known as [[Azariah]], the tenth king of the ancient Kingdom of [[Judah]]. He was 16 when he became king of Judah and reigned for 52 years. The first 24 years of his reign were as a co-regent with his father, [[Amaziah]]. 

Member of the [[Kohathites]], son of [[Uriel]].

Priest of the family of Harim.

Member of the [[Judahites]], father of [[Athaiah]].

Father of [[Jehonathan]].

[[Uzzia]], one of [[David]]'s mighty men.
