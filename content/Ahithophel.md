A counselor of King [[David]] and a man greatly renowned for his wisdom. During [[Absalom]]'s attempt to usurp the throne, he deserted David and supported Absalom, whom he then turned to as an advisor. To counteract his counsel, the fleeing David sent his friend [[Hushai]] back to Absalom. Seeing that his advice against David had not been followed due to Hushai's influence, Ahithophel surmised that the revolt would fail, returned to his hometown of [[Giloh]], and hanged himself. He was buried in "the sepulcher of his fathers".

Father of [[Eliam]].
