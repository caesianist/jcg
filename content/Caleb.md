Son of [[Hezron]], husband of [[Azubah]], then [[Ephrata]].

Son of [[Jephunneh]], father of [[Achsah]] and [[Elah]], a figure who appears in the Hebrew Bible as a representative of the Tribe of [[Judah]] during the [[Israelites]]' journey to the Promised Land.
